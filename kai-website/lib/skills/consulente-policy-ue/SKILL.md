# EU Economics & Politics: Routing Table

## Section 1: Historical Foundations & Institutions (istituzioni-trattati-ue)
**Coverage:** European integration history, treaty evolution, institutional architecture, legal principles
- Treaty of Paris (1951) and ECSC founding
- Rome Treaty (1957) and EEC creation
- Single European Act (1987) and single market goal
- Maastricht Treaty (1993) and monetary union path
- Amsterdam, Nice, Lisbon Treaties (1997–2009)
- EU institutional structure: Commission, Council, Parliament, Court
- Legal system: direct effect, primacy, supremacy, effet utile
- Competence distribution: exclusive, shared, supporting
- Democratic legitimacy and institutional balance

## Section 2: Single Market & Four Freedoms (mercato-unico-4-liberta)
**Coverage:** Internal market mechanics, freedom of movement, harmonization approaches
- Four freedoms: goods, people, services, capital
- Goods: customs union, common external tariff
- Freedom of persons: workers, establishment, services
- Free movement of capital and payments
- Harmonization vs. mutual recognition
- Cassis de Dijon principle
- Dassonville test
- Services and Services Directive
- Digital Single Market dimensions
- State of Union Internal Market

## Section 3: Monetary Policy & Eurozone (politica-monetaria-euro)
**Coverage:** ECB structure, monetary policy instruments, euro functioning, convergence criteria
- European Central Bank (ECB) and ESCB system
- Monetary policy strategy and price stability
- OMT (Outright Monetary Transactions)
- QE (Quantitative Easing) programs
- TLTRO (Targeted Long-Term Refinancing Operations)
- Maastricht convergence criteria
- Sustainability of public finances
- Debt and deficit rules
- Fiscal-monetary policy coordination
- Euro area challenges and crisis management

## Section 4: Economic & Fiscal Governance (governance-economica-fiscale)
**Coverage:** Fiscal rules, governance architecture, crisis mechanisms, recovery programs
- Stability and Growth Pact (SGP)
- Six-Pack and Two-Pack regulatory framework
- Fiscal Compact and Treaty on TSCG
- European Semester and policy coordination
- Macroeconomic imbalance procedures
- Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)
- European Stability Mechanism (ESM)
- SURE (Support to mitigate Unemployment Risks)
- Next Generation EU (NGEU) and Recovery Fund
- National Recovery and Resilience Plans (PNRR)

## Section 5: Sectoral Policies & Market Regulation (politiche-settoriali-ue)
**Coverage:** Agriculture, cohesion, competition, climate, digital governance
- Common Agricultural Policy (CAP)
- Structural and Cohesion Funds
- Regional development and convergence
- Competition policy and antitrust enforcement
- State aid control and exemptions
- European Green Deal
- Climate and energy policy
- Digital Markets Act (DMA)
- Digital Services Act (DSA)
- Market regulation and consumer protection
